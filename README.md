# Dominion

A NeoForge 26.3 Minecraft mod: found a kingdom, recruit villagers, put your people to work, raise an army and claim the land chunk by chunk.

## Getting started
1. Craft a **Town Hall** (planks around a lectern, a gold ingot on top, stone bricks along the bottom) and place it. Name your kingdom, pick a primary and secondary color, then design your flag: a base color plus up to 8 pattern layers, each in its own color (or copy a banner you're holding). The flag flies over the Town Hall and is worn on every soldier's tabard. The ruler can redesign it any time from the Realm screen. You get 20 Gold Coins, a Command Baton and 30 Influence.
2. Put **chests or barrels within 6 blocks of the Town Hall**. They are your storehouse: workers fill them, citizens eat from them, taxes go in and wages come out.
3. Place **beds** on your land (one per citizen), then right-click villagers standing on your land while holding **5 Gold Coins** to recruit them.
4. Hand a citizen a tool to give them a job, right where they stand:
   - Hoe: **Farmer** (harvests and replants crops nearby, sows empty farmland)
   - Axe: **Lumberjack** (fells whole trees nearby and replants them)
   - Sword, spear or any Dominion melee weapon: **Militia**
   - Bow: **Archer**
   - Flintlock or Musket: **Musketeer** (holds at range and fires, then reloads; the kingdom supplies the powder)
   - Builder's Hammer (3 iron ingots over 2 sticks): **Builder**
   Hand them armor and they wear it. Sneak + right-click with an empty hand takes their gear back. Right-click with an empty hand shows their status.
5. Every Minecraft day each citizen eats one food item from the storehouse. Workers pay 1 coin in tax, soldiers draw 1 coin in wages. Three days hungry or unpaid and they leave.
6. Sell surplus at a **Market** block for Gold Coins (right-click it with a stack, or empty-handed for the Market screen).

## Building
- Open the **Build** tab of the Realm screen, pick a structure and take its blueprint: Cottage (2 beds), Barracks (4 beds, flies your flag), Wheat Farm, Watchtower, Storehouse, Market Stall, Wall Gate, Stone Wall, Palisade.
- Right-click the ground with the blueprint to mark it out (an outline shows while you hold it; the white edge is the front). Sneak + right-click to rotate, right-click the same spot again to confirm.
- Walls: click where the wall starts, click where it ends (straight or diagonal, up to 64 blocks, following the ground), then click the end again to confirm.
- Builders fetch materials from the storehouse, clear the site and place every block. Beds, wool and banners come out in your kingdom's colors; any color of bed or wool works as material. They tell you when they're missing something.

## Research and building tiers
- Craft a **Research Table** (lapis, bookshelf, lapis over planks) or build a **Library**, and right-click it to open the Research tab. The ruler picks a research; its Gold Coins and lapis come out of the storehouse.
- Research finishes on its own, slowly. Every **Scholar** (a citizen holding a book) working at a Research Table makes it go faster: 3x with one, up to 7x with three.
- Research unlocks better building materials, in 5 tiers: **Thatch** (mud and hay, available from the start) → **Timber** (Carpentry) → **Stone** (Masonry) → **Brick** (Brickworks) → **Royal** (Royal Architecture: deepslate and copper). It also unlocks the **Blacksmith** (Blacksmithing) and the **University** (Higher Learning).
- Pick the tier in the Build tab before taking a blueprint.
- Every finished building gets a sign by its door. Sneak + right-click the sign to have your builders rebuild it in your best tier. Furniture and chests stay put, and the old materials go to the storehouse.

## Weapons
All crafted from iron ingots and sticks (see the recipe book):
- **Longsword**: a step up from the iron sword, still quick.
- **Greatsword**: slow, huge hits, a little extra reach.
- **Battle Axe**: brutal chops that knock a shield aside for 5 seconds.
- **Halberd**: an axe on a pole: 4.5 block reach, heavy, beats shields.
- **Glaive**: a curved blade on a pole: 4.5 block reach, quicker than the halberd.
- **Flail**: a spiked ball on a chain (iron block + chain) that stuns shields.
- **Iron Shield**: blocks like a shield, lasts twice as long, recovers faster when axed. Blacksmiths now forge these for your soldiers.
- **Flintlock** and **Musket**: hold use to load a **Bullet** (2 iron nuggets + gunpowder + paper makes 8), use again to fire. The Flintlock loads fast and hits for 10; the Musket takes longer, flies farther and straighter, and hits for 16.

## Houses
Four house designs to choose from on the Build tab: the **Cottage**, the long three-bed **Longhouse**, the wide **Farmhouse** with a covered porch, and the narrow, steep-roofed **Townhouse**. They come in every tier. After researching **Upper Storeys**, hold a Builder's Hammer and right-click a house's sign: your builders add a second storey with a ladder up, two more beds and a chest (they take the old roof off first).

Every house gets open shutters beside its windows, flowering planters under the ground-floor windows, lanterns and a path at the front door, and a chimney with a smoking fire. Decorations are free: builders bring their own trim.

## Storage labels
- Craft Storage Labels (paper + string). Right-click a storehouse chest or barrel with one to cycle its category: Food, Farming, Wood, Mining, Building, Armory, Coins & Research, or Anything.
- Workers put items in a matching labeled chest first, then in unlabeled ones, and never in a chest labeled for something else.

## More jobs
- **Scholar** (a book): speeds up research at a Research Table.
- **Blacksmith** (Blacksmith's Tongs, after Blacksmithing): works at an anvil (the Smithy has one). Smelts raw iron, gold and copper from the storehouse with coal, and forges iron armor and shields straight onto soldiers who are missing them.

## University classes
- After Higher Learning, build a University (or craft a Training Desk). Select citizens with the Command Baton, bring them within 12 blocks of the desk and click it to choose a class (paid from the storehouse):
  - **Wizard**: carries a Spell Book and casts from it: fireballs, lines of fangs, lightning at far targets, Frost Nova when enemies crowd in, and Healing Light when friends are hurt.
  - **Barbarian**: 40 health, hits hard, and goes berserk when wounded.
  - **Monk**: fights barehanded, heals nearby allies and steadies them in battle.
  - **Engineer**: builds twice as fast, and inspects finished buildings and repairs any damage.

## Spells
Craft a **Spell Book** (book, 2 lapis, blaze powder, amethyst shard). Right-click to cast; sneak + right-click to turn to the next spell. Spells cost experience points and each has its own cooldown:
Fireball (3 XP), Evoker Fangs (4), Frost Nova (6: freezes and slows enemies around you), Lightning (8, strikes where you look), Healing Light (5: heals you and your people nearby), Blink (4: teleport a short way forward).

## Ammo Pouch
Craft one from leather, string and an iron nugget. It holds 512 Bullets: right-click it to sweep the loose bullets in your inventory into it, sneak + right-click to take 64 out. Flintlocks and Muskets load straight from a pouch anywhere in your inventory.

## The wild

Every mob has a spawn egg (Citizen, Deer, Otter, Orc, Orc Warlord, Illager Overlord, Centaur, Clydesdale, Mustang, Shetland Pony), in the Dominion tab and the Spawn Eggs tab. They work on spawners too.
- **Horses**: three new breeds roam the plains, meadows and taiga. The **Clydesdale** is huge and tough but slow; the **Mustang** is fast and a great jumper; the **Shetland Pony** is tiny and sturdy. Tame, saddle and breed them like horses; foals take after their parents.
- **Deer** live in forests, taiga and meadows. They bolt unless you sneak; tempt and breed them with apples or berries. Stags carry antlers. They drop venison (cook it) and leather.
- **Centaurs** roam the grasslands in bands. They leave you alone unless you hurt one (then the band shoots back), and they hunt orcs.
- **Orcs** live in villages of hide huts around a War Totem, led by a chieftain with a battle axe. They attack players, villagers and citizens. Kill every orc in a village, then right-click the War Totem: the 3x3 chunks around it become yours, with 25 gold coins of plunder.
- **Wild kingdoms**: small towns and great kingdoms are scattered through the world, each with its own name, colours, flag, buildings, people and soldiers (some on patrol). Great kingdoms are built around a **Castle**. Their soldiers defend each other. Defeat every soldier near the Town Hall, then right-click it: the kingdom's land, buildings, storehouse and surviving people become yours.
- Settlements appear in 512-block regions as you explore (none near the world spawn). Operators can raise one with `/dominion admin settle orc|town|kingdom`.

## Workshops
Workshops hire an idle citizen (anyone with empty hands and no University class) and, every minute their worker is on the job in daylight, make goods for your storehouse. Stone and better workshops take two hands, Royal ones three. The **Industry** tab of the Realm screen shows who works where, what each made last and what it's short of, and lets the ruler close a workshop (its workers are let go).
- **Mine** (Mining): stone, coal, iron, copper, lapis, redstone, sometimes gold; now and then a diamond in Stone or better.
- **Lumber Mill** (Carpentry): logs from the storehouse into 6 planks each, plus sticks.
- **Mill** (Milling): 3 wheat into 2 Flour (3 in Stone). **Bakery** (Milling): 1 Flour into 2 bread (3 in Brick), or 3 wheat into 1 bread.
- **Butcher** (Animal Husbandry): breeds the cows, pigs, sheep and chickens within 24 blocks with feed from the storehouse and culls the biggest herd into cooked meat, leather, wool and feathers. Named animals are never touched.
- **Harbor** (Seafaring): fish, and more beside open water; cooked in Stone or better; now and then a treasure.
- **Tavern**: +3 Influence a day, and most mornings a traveller stays on and joins you (if there's a free bed and 10 food in store).
- **Chapel** (Faith): +2 Influence a day; the Priest heals and cures your people within 32 blocks.
- **Archery Range** (Archery): no workers needed. Every archer in the kingdom hits 25% harder (40% in Stone or better).

## Watchtowers
Three levels: the log **Lookout Tower** (no research), the **Stone Watchtower** (Masonry) and the **Fortified Tower** (Fortification). Sneak + right-click a tower's sign to raise it to the next level.
- **Garrison**: 1, 2 or 4 of your archers climb up and keep watch. From the top they shoot further and 25% harder.
- **Early warning**: enemies within 40, 56 or 72 blocks glow, and the bell rings with a message saying what's coming and from where. Armies and war parties marching on you are spotted three times further out.
- **Land**: you can claim chunks up to 1, 2 or 3 chunks around a tower even where they don't touch your borders.
- The **Defense** tab lists your towers and garrisons, every army marching on you, and the orc and Illager camps nearby; towers and their land show on the map.

## The Illager empire
Illager outposts (a dark oak tower, tents and a cage of captive villagers round an **Illager Standard**) appear in the wild. Left alone they grow into walled **forts** (evokers, witches, a ravager pen) and then **citadels**, ruled from a great hall by the **Illager Overlord**, a crowned evoker boss with 300 health who raises rings of fangs, calls vindicator bodyguards and blinks away when cornered.
- At first light a camp may send a **war party** at the nearest town: pillagers and vindicators, then evokers and witches, then a ravager with a rider, and rarely the Overlord himself. They hunt your citizens.
- Clear a camp, then right-click its Standard: the land around it (more for a citadel), coins and emeralds, and the caged captives swear fealty to you (if you have beds).
- Operators: `/dominion admin settle illager`, `/dominion admin citadel`, `/dominion admin illagerraid`.

## The screens
Every screen got a new look (parchment in a dark wood frame, wooden buttons, cloth title ribbons) and more on it:
- **Realm**: Overview with stat cards and warnings; Citizens with jobs and workplaces; Build sorted into Homes, Work, Civic, Military and Walls, with each material shown against what's in your storehouse; Research as cards with costs and progress; the new Industry and Defense tabs; Diplomacy.
- **Market**: right-click a Market with an empty hand for every good's price today (and whether your stores make it a glut or scarce), what you carry and what's in store, a Sell button for each, and your caravans and hired guards.
- **University**, **Founding** and **Flag** screens in the same style.

## Rival kingdoms and diplomacy
- Wild kingdoms are alive: once a day each one may claim more land, put up a new building and train another soldier from its treasury.
- They send **envoys** who ride up to you with offers: **tribute** (pay or face war), **trade**, an **alliance**, or **peace** after a war. Click [Accept] or [Refuse] in chat. Killing an envoy is an act of war.
- The **Diplomacy** tab of the Realm screen lists every kingdom you know, with its strength and your relation (Neutral, Trading, Allied, War), and buttons to declare war, offer trade, propose an alliance, demand tribute or sue for peace.
- At war, a kingdom raises an **army** (knights, horse archers, musketeers, pikemen, archers, militia and siege engines) that marches across the map toward your Town Hall. It appears when you're near; break it and the survivors flee. If it holds your hall it plunders your storehouse and leaves. Peace comes with a truce.
- Allies never hurt each other. Hitting someone from a kingdom you're not at war with is stopped.

## Cavalry and stables
- Research **Horsemanship**, then build **Stables**. They come with a Clydesdale and a Mustang, and every few minutes they saddle a spare kingdom horse for a soldier who needs one (a saddle, or 3 leather and an iron ingot from the storehouse) and breed a new foal (hay or wheat).
- Swordsmen ride **Clydesdales** as knights and **charge**: hits from the saddle at speed do extra damage and knock foes back. Archers ride **Mustangs** as horse archers. Hand a citizen a saddled, tamed horse you're leading to make it theirs.
- **Supply Cart**: use it on a tamed adult Shetland Pony to hitch it up. Near your Town Hall the cart loads arrows, shot and food from the storehouse; out in the field it refills archers and musketeers (they carry 40 shots and fight with a knife when they run dry) and feeds the wounded. Right-click to open it; sneak + right-click to unhitch.

## Sieges
- Research **Siegecraft** for the **Battering Ram** and **Trebuchet**, and **Gunpowder** for the **Cannon** and **Cannonballs**. Place them like spawn eggs; a crew member (any citizen of yours nearby, or you) must stand close for them to work. Load trebuchets with cobblestone and cannons with cannonballs; sneak + right-click to pack one up.
- Command Baton **Siege** mode: right-click a block to have every engine within 64 blocks bombard it; right-click the air to stand them down.
- Rams batter gates and walls; trebuchets lob boulders over them; cannons hit hard and straight. Blocks crack before they break.
- Every block knocked out of your walls is remembered. When the fighting stops, builders and engineers put it back from the storehouse (any stone for stone, any planks for planks). During an attack, archers and musketeers climb onto the walls and man the battlements.

## Orc warbands
- Orc villages grow: after a few days a village becomes a **war camp** with a palisade, then a **stronghold** with a taller palisade, watch platforms and the hut of an **Orc Warlord** (a huge boss with a ground slam, a leap and a war cry that summons more orcs). He drops the Warlord's Cleaver and a big pile of coins.
- At nightfall, war camps and strongholds may send a **raid** at the nearest town. Raiders slink home at dawn.
- Taking a stronghold gives more land and more plunder than a village.

## Trade and roads
- **Roads** are a new wall type in the Build tab: draw a line and builders lay a 3-wide path at ground level (dirt path, coarse dirt, andesite, polished andesite or deepslate by tier). Everyone moves faster on roads.
- With a Market Stall, your kingdom sends **caravans** twice a day to kingdoms you trade with or are allied to, and to your outposts. A merchant leads a pony and cart carrying your surplus; it earns coins at the other end and brings goods home. Caravans travel faster on roads.
- **Bandits** ambush caravans on the open road. Hire guards (2 coins each per trip) on the Market screen with + and -.
- **Market prices follow supply**: whatever your storehouse already has plenty of fetches less, and anything scarce fetches more. The market screen marks each good as glut or scarce.

## Pets
- **Otters** live in families along rivers, swamps and forest lakes. Feed one raw cod or salmon to tame it (one try in three). A tame otter follows you, sits when you right-click it, swims fast, floats on its back in calm water, and every few minutes in the water dives and brings you a fish. Breed otters with fish; pups take after their parents and belong to you.
- **Creature Cage** (sticks, iron bars, planks): right-click your pet, or a small animal, to put it in the cage; right-click the ground to let it out with its name, collar and owner unchanged. It takes cats, dogs, otters, parrots, foxes, rabbits, chickens, axolotls, frogs, armadillos, ocelots, turtles, allays and any pet you've tamed, but not horses or other people's pets.
- **Pet Bed** (wool, a carpet and sticks): at night your tame pets nearby that haven't been told to sit curl up in the nearest free bed (one to a bed) and heal while they sleep. They get up at dawn, or if you go far from home. Use any dye on a bed to recolor it.

## Children
- When there's a free bed and the storehouse has food to spare, two citizens near the Town Hall may have a child. Children join the kingdom, can't work, and grow up after one Minecraft day.

## Beds
- Each citizen gets their own bed automatically (the nearest free one on your land) and sleeps in it at night.
- To move someone: select just them with the Command Baton, then right-click a bed with it. Whoever slept there swaps into their old bed. Right-click a bed with nobody selected to see whose it is.
- The **Citizens** tab of the Realm screen lists everyone with their job and bed.

## Land
- Press **K** (or right-click your Town Hall) for the Realm screen: your stats, a map of nearby chunks, and Claim / Release buttons.
- Click a chunk on the Realm map to claim it, or right-click one of yours to release it (hover for details).
- A new kingdom starts with the 3x3 chunks around its Town Hall. Each extra chunk costs Influence (10, plus 3 more for every chunk beyond the first 9) and it must border your land. Influence comes in through the day: 5 + 3 per citizen.
- Outsiders can't break, place or use blocks on your land. Monster spawning can be switched off for your land.
- Hold the Command Baton to see borders in each kingdom's color.

## Command Baton
- Right-click a citizen: select or deselect. Sneak + right-click a citizen: select every soldier within 24 blocks.
- Right-click the air: give the current order. Sneak + right-click: switch order (**Rally**, **Hold**, **Return to duty**, **Patrol route**, **Clear selection**).
- **Patrol route**: in this mode, right-click blocks to mark up to 16 waypoints (click the last one again to undo), then right-click the air to send your selected soldiers round the route, forever, fighting anything they meet on the way. Give them any other order to stop.
- **Rally**: soldiers fall in behind you, two abreast while you move and in a battle line when you stop.
- Right-click a block: soldiers march there and hold it; selected workers make it their new work spot.
- Right-click an enemy: selected soldiers **charge** it.
- Soldiers fight harder (Strength) near any member of their kingdom. If you die, nearby troops waver and fall back.

## Commands
`/dominion claim | unclaim | realm | list | invite <player> | join | leave | crown <player>`
`/dominion diplomacy <war|peace|trade|alliance|tribute|break> <kingdom>` · `/dominion caravan guards <0-6>`
Operators: `/dominion admin influence <amount>`, `/dominion admin disband <kingdom>`, `/dominion admin settle orc|town|kingdom`, `/dominion admin war <kingdom>`, `/dominion admin envoy <kind> <kingdom>`, `/dominion admin armies`, `/dominion admin raid`, `/dominion admin stronghold`, `/dominion admin settle illager`, `/dominion admin citadel`, `/dominion admin illagerraid`

## Building
Pushes build the jar on GitHub Actions (Build workflow, artifact `dominion-mod`). Textures are painted by `tools/gen_textures.py` and `tools/gen_hd.py` (which also runs `gen_weapons.py` and `gen_wild.py`).
